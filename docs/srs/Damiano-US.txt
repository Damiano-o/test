User Stories (3 individual)

US-1: As a client, I want to order a product directly from a local vendor,
So that I receive fresh food from my territory.
(realized by UC-04 Order a Product)

US-2: As a client, I want to know the total price of my order before
confirming it, So that I can decide whether to proceed.
(realized by UC-04 Order a Product, MSS step 3)

US-3: As a vendor, I want to be notified when a client orders one of my
products, So that I can prepare the delivery in time.
(realized by UC-04 Order a Product, Observer pattern — VenditoreNotifier)
