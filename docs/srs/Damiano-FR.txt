Functional Requirements (3 individual)

FR-1: The system shall create a single direct order for one buyer and one
vendor, tracking the order state transitions defined by the business rules.
(realized by UC-04 Order a Product, BR-04)

FR-2: The system shall verify the availability of a marketplace product
before recording an order, reducing the available quantity and rejecting
the order if the requested quantity exceeds the residual stock.
(realized by UC-04 Order a Product, BR-03/BR-07)

FR-3: The system shall notify the vendor asynchronously when a new order
for one of their products is created.
(realized by UC-04 Order a Product, Observer pattern — VenditoreNotifier)
